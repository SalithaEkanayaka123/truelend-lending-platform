## User Story
As a user, I want Underwriting decision with reason codes so that the milestone advances.

## Business Value
Turns a submitted application into AUTO_APPROVE, AUTO_REJECT, or MANUAL_REVIEW using the policy version pinned at submission, with explainable reason codes.

## Description
Underwriting decision with reason codes

## Scope
**In:** POST /api/applications/{id}/underwrite on a SUBMITTED application, callable once; rule order: income floor, age range, score thresholds; status mapping AUTO_APPROVE->APPROVED, AUTO_REJECT->REJECTED, MANUAL_REVIEW->MANUAL_REVIEW; ApplicationApproved event published in the same transaction on any transition to APPROVED
**Out:** re-underwriting after a policy change

## Acceptance Criteria
- **E3-S3-AC1** — *Given* a submitted PERSONAL application (age 40, income "100000.00", no flags, pinned version 1), *when* underwritten, *then* decision AUTO_APPROVE, score 740, reason_codes ["SCORE_MEETS_AUTO_APPROVE"], policy_version 1, status APPROVED, and one ApplicationApproved event is published.
- **E3-S3-AC2** — *Given* age 30, income "60000.00", no flags (score 680, review band 600-699), *when* underwritten, *then* decision MANUAL_REVIEW with ["SCORE_IN_REVIEW_BAND"] and status MANUAL_REVIEW.
- **E3-S3-AC3** — *Given* age 30, income "30000.00", prior_default (score 500), *when* underwritten, *then* decision AUTO_REJECT with ["SCORE_BELOW_MIN"] and status REJECTED.
- **E3-S3-AC4** — *Given* age 70, income "100000.00" (score 700), *when* underwritten, *then* decision AUTO_REJECT with ["AGE_OUT_OF_RANGE"] only.
- **E3-S3-AC5** — *Given* application A submitted under version 1, version 2 (PERSONAL min_income "30000.00") approved afterwards, and application B submitted after that, both age 30 with income "26000.00", *when* underwritten, *then* A uses policy_version 1 and is MANUAL_REVIEW (score 640), while B uses policy_version 2 and is AUTO_REJECT with ["INCOME_BELOW_MIN"].
- **E3-S3-AC6** — *Given* a DRAFT application, or an already decided one, *when* underwrite is called, *then* the DRAFT case is 409 NOT_SUBMITTED and the already-decided case is 409 ALREADY_DECIDED with the stored decision unchanged.
