## User Story
As a user, I want Minimum-income violation so that the milestone advances.

## Business Value
A hard policy floor on income must reject automatically with a clear, audit-visible reason rather than silently falling through to scoring.

## Description
Minimum-income violation

## Scope
**In:** PolicyViolationException(INCOME_BELOW_MIN) raised by the rule engine below the product's min_income; the underwriting service catching it and persisting AUTO_REJECT/INCOME_BELOW_MIN
**Out:** any other policy-violation category

## Acceptance Criteria
- **E3-S4-AC1** — *Given* PERSONAL with min_income "25000.00", *when* the rule engine evaluates income "24999.99", *then* it raises PolicyViolationException with code INCOME_BELOW_MIN.
- **E3-S4-AC2** — *Given* income exactly "25000.00" (age 30, no flags, score 640), *when* evaluated, *then* no exception is raised and the decision is MANUAL_REVIEW.
- **E3-S4-AC3** — *Given* a submitted application with income "24999.99", *when* underwrite is called, *then* it is 200 with AUTO_REJECT, reason_codes ["INCOME_BELOW_MIN"], status REJECTED, and a persisted decisions row.
