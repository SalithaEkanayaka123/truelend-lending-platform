## Description
Credit-scoring stub

## Scope
**In:** a pure function score(age, income, credit_flags) -> integer; fixed formula: base 600 + age points + income points - flag penalties
**Out:** real bureau data; editable score weights

## Acceptance Criteria
- **E3-S2-AC1** — *Given* age 40, income "100000.00", no flags, *when* scored, *then* the score is 740.
- **E3-S2-AC2** — *Given* age 30 with income "60000.00" and no flags, or age 30 with income "30000.00" and prior_default, *when* scored, *then* the first scores 680 and the second scores 500.
- **E3-S2-AC3** — *Given* the same inputs, *when* scored 100 times, *then* all 100 scores are identical.
- **E3-S2-AC4** — *Given* the 680 baseline, *when* one input changes at a time, *then* age 40 gives 700, income "70000.00" gives 690, missed_payment_12m gives 620, thin_file gives 640, and prior_default gives 530.
- **E3-S2-AC5** — *Given* age 18, income "0.00" and all three flags, or age 40 with income "150000.00" or "500000.00", *when* scored, *then* the first scores 350 and both of the second case scores are 790.
